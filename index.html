Commercial use

CREATING IT AND LICENSING IT ARE TWO DIFFERENT THINGS.

A saved Recipe is your reusable artwork setup, not an automatic commercial licence. Production export and commercial capability depend on the appropriate account entitlement and the rights agreed for the specific use.

Need to sell, manufacture, install or use a work commercially?
Tell us what you want to do. Normal commercial use can follow the current approved route. Exclusivity, territory, manufacturing rights, white-label work and unusual large-scale arrangements need a separate written agreement.
ASK ABOUT COMMERCIAL USE

How a paid order works

  1. Tell us what you need.
  2. We confirm scope, price and rights.
  3. You approve the order.
  4. Payment is handled by invoice / agreed business payment method.
  5. We deliver the agreed final files and written usage rights.
