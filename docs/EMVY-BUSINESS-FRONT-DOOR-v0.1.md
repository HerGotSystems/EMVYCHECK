## Canvas Grid quick guide

Keep this visible and beginner-friendly:

1. **Motif** — choose the starting visual structure.
2. **Format** — choose the panel/layout shape.
3. **Composition** — use one motif or combine structures.
4. **Look** — change visual treatment without replacing the composition.
5. **Colour** — audition colour directions without destroying the artwork.
6. **Seed** — explore relatives of the same idea.
7. **Preview** — see it as panels/in a room. Preview is a mock-up, not the production file.
8. **Save Recipe** — save the editable/replayable setup. A Recipe is not a commercial licence.
9. **Advanced / Colour + Layers** — go deeper only when wanted.
