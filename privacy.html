Privacy

WHAT WE DO WITH WHAT YOU SEND US.

This page explains, factually, what the current EMVY CHECK website actually does. It is not a general legal privacy policy for every possible future feature — it describes today's site.

What we collect

Information you voluntarily send us through the contact form or by email — for example your name, email address, organisation, country, and the message you write. We do not run visitor tracking/analytics scripts of our own beyond what our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may log to operate the site.

Why we collect it

To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, process orders, provide support, and handle other business communication with you.

AI assistance

As already disclosed elsewhere on this site, AI tools may be used to help read, organise or draft responses to messages, so the business can keep moving while Michal is working, travelling or away from a computer. Important pricing exceptions, licences, exclusivity, partnerships and other material commercial decisions remain human-approved.

Third-party form delivery

The contact form on this site currently uses FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, to deliver your message to our inbox. If you would rather not use the form, you can email us directly instead.

What not to send us

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information through the contact form or by email.

Questions

For any privacy question, contact emvycheck@gmail.com.
