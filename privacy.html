Privacy

WHAT WE DO WITH WHAT YOU SEND US.

This page explains, factually, what the current EMVY CHECK website actually does. It is not a general legal privacy policy for every possible future feature — it describes today's site and today's workflow.

Who is responsible for your information

EMVY CHECK, run by Michal Veltruský, is responsible for the information described on this page. For any privacy question, contact emvycheck@gmail.com directly — that is also the address the contact form delivers to.

What we collect

Information you voluntarily send us through the contact form or by email — for example your name, email address, organisation, country, the message you write, and any link you choose to share. We do not run visitor tracking/analytics scripts of our own, beyond what our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may log to operate and secure the site.

Why we collect it, and on what basis

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss and prepare a quote, take the steps you ask for before an order becomes a firm agreement, fulfil an order you confirm, and provide ordinary support and business communication. Where you are asking about a possible order, we handle your details on the basis that it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and — once you confirm an order — to perform that contract. For a general enquiry that never becomes an order, we handle your details on the basis that it is in our legitimate interest, and yours, to respond to you. Where the law requires us to keep certain records (for example for accounting or tax purposes), we keep the minimum needed to meet that legal obligation.

We do not use your information to make any automated decision about you — every enquiry, quote and order is read and decided on by a human.

AI assistance

As already disclosed elsewhere on this site, AI tools may be used to help read, organise or draft responses to messages, so the business can keep moving while Michal is working, travelling or away from a computer. When that happens, the content of your message may be processed by the AI service provider used for that purpose, only as part of preparing a response. Important pricing exceptions, licences, exclusivity, partnerships and other material commercial decisions remain human-approved — AI assistance does not decide these for you.

Who else sees this information

We do not sell or trade your information. The services currently involved in running this site and handling messages are:

  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and its contact form page;
  • FormSubmit — a third-party form-delivery service that relays a contact-form submission to our inbox (see below);
  • Gmail / Google — the email service our business inbox, emvycheck@gmail.com, runs on;
  • an AI service provider, only for the specific messages where AI assistance is actually used as described above.

Third-party form delivery

The contact form on this site currently uses FormSubmit to deliver your message to our inbox. If you would rather not use the form, you can email us directly instead.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order correspondence for as long as it is genuinely useful to the business relationship (for example, so we can find your previous order if you write again), and for as long as we are legally required to keep records such as invoices. We do not currently have a fixed retention schedule beyond that; if you would like us to delete correspondence that we are not legally required to keep, contact us and we will do so.

Language preference (local storage)

If you use the quick-guide language panel on this site, your chosen language is remembered using your browser's own local storage, on your device only. This is not an advertising or tracking cookie, it is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is not used to profile you — it simply lets the panel reopen in the language you last picked.

What not to send us

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information through the contact form or by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you are and which law applies, you may have rights to ask us, where applicable: to see the information we hold about you; to correct it if it is wrong; to delete it; to restrict or object to how we use it; and to receive a copy in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact emvycheck@gmail.com.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Questions

For any privacy question, contact emvycheck@gmail.com.
